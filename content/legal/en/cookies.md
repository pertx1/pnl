# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: [FECHA]_

> Draft pending review by a lawyer. Do not publish without review.

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser's local storage) are used by [DOMINIO] and the Kreo application, provided by [RAZÓN SOCIAL] ([NIF]).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information between visits or pages. Local storage (localStorage) is a similar technology that stores data in your browser.

## 2. Cookies we use

**We currently only use strictly necessary cookies and storage** to provide the service you request. They are all first-party (Kreo's own) and exempt from consent under Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI). We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, nor third-party cookies.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-*` (e.g. `sb-<project>-auth-token`) | First-party, technical cookie | Keep you securely signed in (Supabase Auth) | Up to 400 days or until you sign out |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | First-party, technical cookie (requested personalisation) | Remember the language you chose | 1 year |
| `kreo_ws` | First-party, technical, httpOnly cookie | Remember the active workspace | 1 year |
| `kreo_consent` | First-party, technical cookie | Store your cookie preferences so we don't ask again | [DURATION, proposed: 12 months] |
| `kreo_oauth_*` | First-party, technical, httpOnly cookie | Protect the social account connection process (temporary OAuth sign-in state, protection against request forgery) | 10 minutes |
| `theme` (localStorage) | First-party local storage | Remember whether you prefer light or dark mode | Until you clear your browser data |

## 3. Cookies requiring consent

We currently use none. If we add analytics, non-requested personalisation, advertising or affiliate cookies in the future, **we will ask for your prior consent** through the cookie banner, with options to accept, reject or configure with equal ease, and we will not set them until you accept. We will update this table beforehand.

## 4. Third-party services

Fonts are served from our own server, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts. The sign-in pages of the platforms you connect (TikTok, Meta, Google, Microsoft, Shopify) open on their own domains and use their own cookies, governed by their policies. Payment takes place on Stripe pages, which use their own cookies to process payment and prevent fraud ([Stripe cookie policy](https://stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal)).

## 5. How to manage or delete cookies

You can change your preferences at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings:

- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

If you block technical cookies, you will not be able to sign in or use the application.
