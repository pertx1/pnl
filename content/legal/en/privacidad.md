## 2. Our principles

- **Data minimisation**: we only request from each platform the permissions needed for the features you enable, and only store what is essential to provide them.
- **Official APIs only**: we connect to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Gmail, Outlook and Shopify exclusively through their official APIs and official sign-in (OAuth). We never ask for your password for those platforms or access them through scraping.
- **AI proposes, you decide**: no AI-generated content is published or sent without your explicit approval.
- **Data in the EU**: our database and application are hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Some providers may process data outside the EU with the safeguards described in section 9.
- **Security**: access tokens for your connected accounts are stored encrypted, data access is isolated per workspace and our technical logs contain no unnecessary personal data.

## 4. Connected accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Gmail, Outlook, Shopify)

When you connect an account, the platform shows you its own official authorisation screen with the requested permissions. You decide whether to grant them.

- **What we store**: the account ID, username, display name, avatar, granted permissions, connection status and access and refresh tokens.
- **Encrypted tokens**: tokens are stored encrypted in our EU database and are only used by our server for the actions you request. They are never exposed to the browser or shared with third parties.
- **Content and metrics**: we only obtain from each platform what is needed for the feature you use (for example, your list of posts and their metrics for statistics). Metrics are refreshed periodically and replaced by new data; we do not keep more history than needed to show the trends you asked for.
- **Publishing**: when you schedule a post, we store the file, caption and settings until it is published (or you cancel it) and for [PERIOD, proposed: 30 days] afterwards so you can check the result; then the file is deleted and only the post record (ID, date and status) is kept.
- **Disconnecting**: you can disconnect any account from Settings > Connected accounts. When you do, we revoke the tokens with the platform where it allows us to, delete them from our database and delete the data obtained from that account within [PERIOD, proposed: 7] days, except data we must keep by law.
- **Platform limits**: we respect each API's usage limits and do not automate interactions (likes, follows, comments or bulk messages).

### 4.1 Google and YouTube

Kreo uses the **YouTube API Services** to connect your YouTube channel, upload the videos you schedule, read your channel and video information and show their statistics. By using the YouTube features in Kreo you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's processing of your data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- **Data we access**: channel information (ID, name, image), list of videos and their metadata, channel and video statistics (YouTube Analytics) and the ability to upload videos on your behalf when you schedule them.
- **How we use it**: solely to show you your information and statistics within Kreo and to publish the content you have approved.
- **Who we share it with**: no one, except the technical sub-processors needed to provide the Service (hosting and database, see section 8) and where required by law. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
- **Storage**: we store the data in our EU database. Data obtained from the API is refreshed or deleted within the periods required by the YouTube developer policies (non-authorised data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days).
- **Cookies and device**: Kreo does not place or read cookies on your device through the YouTube API Services, nor store device information for that purpose. See the [Cookie Policy]([DOMINIO]/legal/cookies).
- **How to revoke access**: you can disconnect YouTube from Settings > Connected accounts, or revoke Kreo's access at any time via the Google security settings page: [https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). When you revoke access, we delete the associated YouTube data within 7 days at most.
- **Public channel analysis**: for competitor analysis we query public YouTube channel data through the YouTube Data API without accessing any private account.

**Limited Use disclosure (Google API Services User Data Policy)**:
Kreo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, with respect to Google data (including Gmail and YouTube):

- We only use it to provide or improve user-facing features within Kreo.
- We do not transfer it to third parties, except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with appropriate safeguards.
- We do not use it for advertising, personalised or otherwise, and we do not sell it.
- We do not use it to develop, train or improve generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models, nor do we allow our AI providers to do so.
- No human reads this data, except with your explicit consent for specific messages, where necessary for security purposes (for example, investigating abuse), to comply with the law, or when aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

### 6.1 AI assistants you connect to Kreo (MCP server and "connected apps")

You can connect a third-party AI assistant (for example, Claude by Anthropic) to Kreo through our MCP server, with an OAuth authorization you grant on a Kreo consent screen or with a personal API key.

- **What we store**: the name declared by the app, the address it returns to, the workspace you choose, the connection and last-use dates, and the access credentials **only as a cryptographic digest (hash)**, never in clear text. To prevent abuse we rate-limit requests per user or per a truncated digest of the IP address (we do not store the IP), deleted within 2 days at most.
- **What the app receives**: only what it requests through the MCP tools within the authorized workspace and with your role (projects, calendar, tasks, your accounts' metrics, Shopify sales, saved competitors and products, AI results). From then on, processing by that app's provider is governed by its own terms and privacy policy: the disclosure is made at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **What it cannot do**: approve, publish or send anything; everything stays as a draft or pending your approval in Kreo. It can spend workspace credits on AI generations if you have the editor role.
- **Revoking**: in Settings > API keys & MCP > Connected apps, with immediate effect. Revoked authorizations are deleted after 30 days and expired ones the day after they expire. Connected apps are included in the data export and deleted with your account.

## 11. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

- **In the app**: in Settings > Account you can **export your data** (downloadable file in a structured format) and **delete your account**. When you delete your account, we revoke the tokens of all connected accounts with each platform, delete your data according to section 10 and cancel your subscription.
- **By email**: write to [EMAIL DPD/PRIVACIDAD] stating the right you wish to exercise. If we have reasonable doubts about your identity, we may ask for additional information.
- **Timeframe**: we will reply within one month, extendable by two further months in complex cases (we will let you know).

If you are a third party whose data is processed by a Kreo customer (for example, someone who sent a message to a user), you can contact that customer as controller; if you write to us, we will help you reach them.

If you believe we have not handled your rights properly, you can lodge a complaint with the **Spanish Data Protection Agency** (AEPD), C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid, [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es), or with the supervisory authority of your EU country of residence.

## 12. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including: encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, specific encryption of third-party tokens, per-workspace data isolation through row-level security policies, role-based access control, available two-factor authentication, backups, secrets managed outside the source code and technical logs without unnecessary personal data. If a security breach poses a risk to you, we will notify the AEPD within 72 hours and, where appropriate, you.
